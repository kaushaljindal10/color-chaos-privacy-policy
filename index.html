Last updated: October 12, 2026

Color Chaos is designed to be an offline game. We do not intentionally collect, request, or store personal information through gameplay.

We do not intentionally sell or share personal information collected through the game. If third-party services included in the app process data, their practices may also apply.

The game is intended to be playable offline. The app may include third-party software components. Their data practices should be reviewed if those components are enabled or used.

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected through the game, please contact us.

5. Data Security and Retention

We do not operate a game account system or intentionally collect personal information through gameplay. Any information stored locally by the game remains on the user's device unless removed by the user or by uninstalling the game.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer, DevHarbor, through the contact details provided on the game's Google Play listing.
